# Deal Pipeline

**Description:** Tracks commitment follow-through for key relationships
**Icon:** 🤝
**Data Sources:** commitments, tasks, messages
**Frequency:** daily

## Tenets
- Relationships are built on follow-through, not intentions
- A commitment older than 7 days without progress is a trust risk
- The person you owe the most to is the one to prioritize today
- Silence after a commitment is worse than saying no

## Prompt
Look at open commitments and recent message activity. Identify the person with the most
outstanding commitments where there has been no recent message exchange.
If someone has 3+ open items, that's a relationship at risk.
Suggest one specific follow-up action for today — name the person and the commitment.
Rules: No emojis, no fluff, maximum 2 sentences.
Respond with ONLY the coaching insight text.
