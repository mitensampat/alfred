on privacy

your data, your machine.

stays on your Mac

every message, contact, calendar event, note. alfred reads them locally and never uploads them.

leaves your Mac

only the snippets alfred needs to think with — sent to Claude (by Anthropic) to generate coaching, then forgotten. no chat logs. no training. no human review.

disconnect any source in settings. delete the app, and every trace goes with it.
