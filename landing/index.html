What Alfred is for

The work that matters usually isn't on a list.

Alfred is built around three things operators kept asking for.

i.

Stop dropping commitments.

Alfred reads your threads across iMessage, WhatsApp, and email, spots promises you made — inbound and outbound — and surfaces what's rotting before it costs a relationship.

ii.

Spend your hours on what compounds.

Every morning, a coached briefing names the one lever — not the todo list. An afternoon check-in asks whether today was spent on what mattered, learned over weeks.

iii.

Name what you're avoiding.

The hard conversation you've been delaying. The email sitting in drafts for three weeks. The feedback you haven't given. Alfred notices the patterns of avoidance — and nudges, privately.

iv.

A coach in the voice you choose.

Alfred ships with a Coaching Skills library — distinct methods and voices you can turn on or off. Pick the coach you'd actually listen to. Gentle at first. Direct when you've earned it.
